How the recommendations work

The idea

The system studies thousands of past shopping baskets and finds items that are often bought together. It saves these patterns as association rules, such as {chawal} → {cooking oil}. When you add items to your cart, it looks up the matching rules and suggests the items that follow.

Algorithms

Apriori builds frequent itemsets level by level and prunes rare candidates. FP-Growth compresses the data into a tree and avoids candidate generation, which makes it faster at low support. Both find identical itemsets; see the Analytics page for the runtime comparison.

Metrics
  • Support: how often the items appear together across all baskets.
  • Confidence: of the baskets with the cart item, how many also had the suggested item.
  • Lift: how much more likely the pair is than by chance. Above 1 means a real positive link.
Two lists

Frequently bought together uses rules only, ranked by confidence. Top picks blends rule confidence with each item's overall popularity, which scored best in our evaluation.

Data

The Groceries dataset (14,758 real baskets) plus 300 synthetic baskets of local items (chai, chawal, daal and others) added for demonstration.
